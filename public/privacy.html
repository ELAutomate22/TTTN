Privacy on this site

This website does not use an analytics service, advertising tracker, account system, or quiz leaderboard. Quiz answers and scores remain in your browser while the page is open; they are not sent to our server.

Contact

The contact forms open an email draft in your own email application. The website does not transmit or store your form entries. If you choose to send the email, the recipient is hello@talktothenation.com, and your email provider’s handling of messages applies.

External services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Video links to Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, and TTTN’s original website open those services, which have their own privacy practices. The homepage video is served as a file from this website.

Updates

This description applies to the current site build. If analytics, embedded media, or server-side forms are added later, this page must be updated before those features are enabled.
